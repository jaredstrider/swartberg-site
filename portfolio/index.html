Portfolio construction

How the portfolio is constructed

The firm's research is combined into one portfolio. Each line of research is held at a size that follows the evidence for it and the conditions it was tested in, and the portfolio is adjusted as those conditions change. The illustration below shows the method on a stylised market path.

Illustration. A stylised market path, not market history. Each regime has its own drift and volatility: calm growth rises steadily, rising inflation trades sideways with more variation, stress falls quickly as volatility rises, recovery regains part of the fall with volatility still high, and calm growth then resumes below the previous peak. The portfolio above follows the cycle and adjusts its lines as conditions change; pause it, or click a regime, to hold one.
