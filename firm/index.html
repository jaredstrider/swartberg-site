The firm

About Swartberg Capital

Swartberg Capital is a systematic research and trading firm in South Africa. It is owned and directed by its two principals, who set the questions the research asks, read the evidence, and take its decisions, and who trade only their own capital. The firm's technology, including artificial intelligence, extends their capacity to do that work. Data, models, and results are held under version control, and every figure the firm uses can be reproduced from the underlying record.

Standards

The standard the firm holds itself to

Sources
Every source cited in the firm's research has been opened and checked against the claim it supports.
Tests
Every test is run under rules written before it begins, on data the rule has not seen, with the number of attempts recorded and with costs stated.
Provenance
Every figure can be traced to the record it came from.
Performance
The firm publishes no performance.
Disclosure
Were results ever presented, they would carry the disclosures a regulated manager uses: hypothetical results labelled as such, costs stated, and no simulated figure described as a return earned. A live record would be verified independently before the principals relied on it.
